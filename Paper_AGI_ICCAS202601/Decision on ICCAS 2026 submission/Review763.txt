Reviewer 2 of ICCAS 2026 submission 346

Comments to the author
======================

- In 4.2 Experimental Design first paragraph, there is no
period in last sentence.

- LLM-based search method’s handling of candidates that
exceed the search bounds should be described.

- The current experiments are conducted under each method
and evaluated over only five independent repetitions.
Therefore, it is necessary to examine whether five
repetitions are sufficient to support the claimed
performance differences between LLM-based search and
Bayesian optimization.
